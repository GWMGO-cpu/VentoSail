corporate_fare Who we are

A freight partner for importers who need China-side control before cargo reaches the port.

Overseas importers often lose time between supplier readiness, pickup arrangements, container planning, warehouse handoff, and document updates. Ventosail is positioned to make those origin-side details easier to coordinate from China.

The company focuses on practical execution rather than vague global claims: understand the cargo, choose the right freight mode, align the supplier and trucking handoff, and keep the importer informed as the shipment moves.

Three freight services, one China-side operating team.

Ventosail presents its service scope clearly so overseas buyers know when to use FCL, when LCL is more practical, and how trucking connects factory pickup to the ocean freight plan.

FCL Ocean Freight

Best fit
20GP, 40GP, and 40HQ cargo needing dedicated container space.
Origin support
Supplier readiness, trucking, port handoff, and sailing coordination.
Coverage
Europe, North America, Middle East, and Africa.

LCL Consolidation

Best fit
Smaller cargo volumes measured by cartons, pallets, CBM, and weight.
Origin support
Pickup, warehouse handoff, consolidation records, and shipment milestones.
Coverage
Flexible ocean freight from China to major destination markets.

Trucking & Inland Transport

Best fit
Factory pickup, port drayage, and origin-side cargo handoff.
Origin support
Pickup scheduling, supplier communication, vehicle planning, and handoff details.
Connected with
FCL and LCL shipments that start at Chinese factories or suppliers.
