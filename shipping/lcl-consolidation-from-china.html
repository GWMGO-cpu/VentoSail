What is LCL shipping?

Move smaller shipments without booking a full container.

LCL means less-than-container-load. Your cargo shares container space with other shipments, making it a flexible option when your volume is not large enough for FCL.

payments

Cost-effective for smaller cargo

LCL helps you avoid paying for unused container space when shipment volume is measured in cartons, pallets, or modest CBM.

Flexible shipment volume

Importers can ship smaller batches more frequently, which can help control inventory without waiting to fill a container.

LCL FAQ

Common LCL questions from importers.

These answers help customers decide whether LCL is the right fit.

When should I choose LCL instead of FCL?

Choose LCL when your cargo volume is too small for a full container or when you want to ship smaller batches more frequently.

What details are needed for an LCL quote?

Origin, destination, cargo description, number of cartons or pallets, CBM, gross weight, ready date, and supplier contact details.

Can Ventosail handle supplier pickup?

Yes. We can coordinate supplier pickup or warehouse handoff in China as part of the LCL shipment flow.

Is LCL slower than FCL?

LCL may require additional consolidation and deconsolidation steps, so planning cargo readiness and documentation early is important.

Need to ship smaller cargo from China?

Send your CBM, weight, origin, and destination details. Ventosail will help you shape the right LCL plan.
