What is FCL shipping?

Use a full container when your cargo needs dedicated space and cleaner control.

FCL means your shipment uses an entire container instead of sharing space with other shippers. It is often the better choice when cargo volume is larger, schedules matter, or you want fewer handling points.

savings

Better unit cost at volume

When your cargo can fill a meaningful portion of a container, FCL can reduce the cost per CBM compared with smaller consolidated shipments.

Dedicated container movement

Your cargo moves in its own sealed container, reducing mixed-cargo handling and making shipment control easier for your team.

Cleaner schedule planning

FCL FAQ

Common FCL questions from importers.

These answers help buyers understand the basics before they request a quote.

When should I choose FCL instead of LCL?

Choose FCL when cargo volume is large enough to justify dedicated container space, or when you want fewer handling points and better shipment control.

What information is needed for an FCL quote?

Origin address, destination, cargo description, gross weight, CBM, container type if known, ready date, and any pickup or special handling requirements.

Can Ventosail arrange factory pickup?

Yes. Trucking coordination and supplier-side handoff are part of the FCL operating flow for China-origin shipments.

Which containers are commonly used?

The most common FCL container types are 20GP, 40GP, and 40HQ. The right option depends on cargo volume, weight, and loading requirements.
