Trucking FAQ

Common trucking questions from importers.

These answers make the China-origin pickup process easier for overseas buyers to understand.

What pickup details do you need?

Supplier address, contact person, cargo description, carton or pallet count, CBM, gross weight, ready date, and destination handoff point.

Can trucking be connected to FCL or LCL?

Yes. Trucking is often part of the larger freight plan, moving cargo from supplier to port, warehouse, or consolidation point.

Can you coordinate with my Chinese supplier?

Yes. Ventosail can communicate with the supplier to confirm readiness, pickup timing, and handoff details.

Do you provide trucking updates?

Yes. We provide practical milestone updates such as pickup scheduled, truck dispatched, pickup completed, and handoff completed.

Need pickup or port trucking in China?

Send supplier address, cargo details, and handoff requirements. Ventosail will help coordinate the origin-side movement.
