About Us

VentoSail is a China-based freight forwarder serving Europe, Africa, the Middle East, and the Americas. We work with major carriers and local brokerage/last-mile partners to deliver one-stop logistics solutions—FCL/LCL, trucking, customs clearance, warehousing, and door-to-door delivery—with transparent milestones and predictable lead time.

  • Core markets: Europe · Africa · Middle East · Americas
  • Service models: FCL / LCL / Door-to-Door / Intermodal
  • Assurance: SLA-based lead time, live status visibility, compliance-first operations
