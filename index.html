What information is needed for a quote?

Origin city or supplier address, destination, cargo description, cartons or pallets, gross weight, CBM, ready date, and preferred service.

Should I choose FCL or LCL?

FCL is usually better when cargo volume justifies a dedicated container. LCL fits smaller shipments that can be consolidated with other cargo.

Can Ventosail coordinate supplier pickup?

Yes. Trucking and pickup coordination are part of the main service scope, especially for China-origin shipments.

Which destination markets are covered?

The current focus is Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Africa, with FCL, LCL, and trucking support matched to shipment needs.
